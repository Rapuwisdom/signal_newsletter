🔥 Top Story

Anthropic just overtook OpenAI in valuation

Anthropic has raised $65 billion in a new funding round, pushing its valuation to $965 billion — officially overtaking OpenAI's $852 billion private market valuation for the first time. That's nearly a trillion-dollar AI safety company. The round includes a $36 billion chip debt deal tied to Google TPUs. Both Anthropic and OpenAI have now filed for IPOs, with OpenAI expected to list in September and Anthropic in October. The race to be the first frontier AI lab in public markets is officially on.

🛠 Tools & Releases

GitHub Copilot goes token-based — and developers are furious

Starting June 1, GitHub Copilot dropped its flat subscription model and switched to token-based billing. The backlash was immediate. Developers report their $29/month bills could balloon to $750/month under the new system. The change charges for actual compute consumed — meaning heavy users of advanced reasoning models get hit hardest. If you use Copilot for work, it's worth checking your usage settings now.

🌍 Industry

Microsoft Build 2026: AI Agents take centre stage

Microsoft Build opened this week in San Francisco with one clear message — AI agents are the future of software. Copilot agents, Azure AI Foundry updates, and deep Windows AI integrations dominated the keynote. Separately, the US Department of Defense awarded Microsoft a $9.69 billion enterprise contract covering Microsoft 365, Azure, and Copilot services — expected to save the DoD $422 million over previous arrangements.
